Who is it for?

Small bakeries, catering operations, independent food processors, shops packaging their own products, and businesses marking cartons or batches may benefit from manual date coding. Confirm the particular unit's font size, ink adhesion and allowed print surface with the seller.

Before you buy

Is the date automatically updated every day?

No. Low-cost handheld coders are manually adjusted. The operator must set the correct date before each batch.

Does it print directly on food?

This page refers to suitable exterior packaging. Do not assume that an ink is approved for direct food contact. Confirm the manufacturer's ink and surface compatibility.

Does stamping a different date extend a product's shelf life?

No. The responsible food business must determine an accurate date. Canadian rules distinguish best-before dates from expiration dates and prohibit misleading date changes.
