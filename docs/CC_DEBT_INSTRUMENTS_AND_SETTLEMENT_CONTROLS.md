# Carnival Careers — Debt Instruments, Promissory Notes & Settlement Control
**Effective operating policy | October 11, 2026 | Toronto-first | applies to all city SPVs**

## Purpose and scope
This is a legal/treasury control inside the existing CC **Profit + Control**, property acquisition, capital and accounts-receivable workflows. It is not a new financing company, investment offering, invitation to buy notes, or substitute for counsel. No new automation/outreach worker is authorized by this document.

### Canada-first law (verify jurisdiction and effective rules on each transaction)
- Bills of Exchange Act, s. 16: a bill of exchange is an unconditional **order** for payment of a sum certain on demand or at a fixed/determinable future time. Source: https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/B-4/section-16.html
- Bills of Exchange Act, s. 176: a promissory note is an unconditional **promise**, signed by maker, to pay a sum certain on demand or at fixed/determinable future time; an accompanying collateral pledge does not by itself invalidate the note. Source: https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/B-4/section-176.html
- Bills of Exchange Act, ss. 34–35, 126–127: formal drawee acceptance matters; an unaccepted bill does not by itself assign drawee funds or make that drawee liable. Source: https://laws.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/B-4/fulltext.html
- Bills of Exchange Act, ss. 80–81 and 95–96: dishonour can create rights of recourse and notice duties against parties legally liable **on the genuine instrument**. A dishonour is NOT an asset, money, account deposit, payment acceptance or a financing facility. Source: https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/B-4/FullText.html
- Ontario securities law may regulate promissory notes as securities, secured or not. A bond, debenture or note is not automatically exempt; the actual offering, trading/registration and prospectus/exemption requirements must be checked by qualified Ontario counsel. Sources: https://www.fsrao.ca/industry/mortgage-brokering/compliance-and-other-resources/promissory-notes-may-be-securities-regulated-ontario-securities-commission-what-you-need-know and https://www.fsrao.ca/industry/mortgage-brokering/regulatory-framework/supervision/dealing-promissory-notes-could-land-you-hot-water-osc-and-fsra
- Criminal Code s. 362 expressly addresses obtaining credit by fraud and knowingly false financial statements connected with a loan, note, cheque, bill or draft. Both notes and bills can be abused; mere issuance is not immunity. Source: https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-46/section-362.html
- **APR / criminal interest:** Criminal Code s.347 generally sets the criminal-interest definition at over 35% actuarial APR. Under the Criminal Interest Rate Regulations effective 2025-01-01, qualifying loans to non-individual corporate/commercial borrowers of **more than C$10,000 up to C$500,000** are exempt from s.347 only at **no more than 48% actuarially computed APR**; loans above C$500,000 have different treatment under the regulations. Broad interest definition can encompass associated fees/commissions and related agreements; the entire actual arrangement, repayment tenor and borrower/collateral exemptions must be reviewed. No public investment coupon or bridge pricing may be prepared without a defensible annual percentage calculation and lawyer signoff. A C$6k financing charge on C$30k for ~24 days is **rejected** and must not be shown as an eligible lender promise. Sources: https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2024-114/section-1.html and https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-46/section-347.html

## Four approved transaction lanes

**1. Seller Vendor-Take-Back debt / property acquisition.** A real seller and buyer negotiate a lawful deferred purchase-price or funded loan obligation through a written, reviewed agreement. Counsel validates parties and authority, exact purchase price, down payment, debt amount, interest, amortization, payment timing, default/cure, maturity, registered charge/mortgage and security priority, land title, tax, closing/trust and independently confirmed lender consent where applicable. The buyer's promissory note evidences debt; it is not cash received or an automatic substitute for a mortgage. Current 35 Tournament Drive inputs are WORKING ONLY; do not state a signed VTB, property control or closed financing without executed paper and disbursement.

**2. Purchase of actual performing mortgage notes.** Buy only demonstrably owned, legally assignable debt/secured-mortgage interests from the actual lawful holder, with a verified note, original mortgage/charge or other security, title search, outstanding principal, interest and arrears, servicing/payment history, borrower rights and notices, priority, assignment/registration, perfection as needed, foreclosure/enforcement feasibility, taxes/fees and market-discount analysis. Model payments, prepayment/default and expenses; do not claim that note ownership makes CC a licensed bank.

**3. Real commercial receivable assignment/discounting.** A signed, lawful vendor/sponsor/content-services contract, performed deliverable, undisputed invoice and counterparty's confirmation may support third-party receivables finance if accepted by a real factor/lender under proper assignment terms. Follow debtor consent and anti-assignment restrictions. Track collections through authorized accounts, remittance and lien release. A purchase order, unsigned LOI, hypothetical sponsor contribution or vendor inquiry is not an account receivable.

**4. Accepted ordinary bill/draft or institutional settlement.** Use only as a payment or collection method within a real underlying obligation where the drawer is authorized, the drawee is a real party, there is actual acceptance/authority as required, and counsel/bank/accounting approves the process. It is not a device for ordering a bank, government, court, trustee or other stranger to pay CC. Do not issue, deposit, endorse, discount, sell or represent any unsupported unilateral bill as money.

## Hard stops
- No 'private acceptance', 'accepted for value', 'dishonour funds the instrument', 'straw man trust', 'court account', 'bonded birth certificate', fictitious asset accounts, 'commercial lien from nonresponse', compelled bank or government payment, or other unsupported settlement mythology.
- Do not draw on the bank account, letter of credit, public fund or obligation of any third party without genuine authority.
- Do not assert a bond and promissory note are differentiated only by jurisdiction, or that either is inherently safe/legal to distribute publicly.
- No intentional false statement about collateral, title, creditworthiness, investor return, funds held or ability to pay.
- No unsanctioned public bond/note issuance, broad investor solicitations, promises of 'double money', offering documents or transfers before corporate, Ontario securities, tax and independent legal review.
- No automatic legal notice/dishonour/collection demand, lien registration, note creation, endorsement, loan application, bank deposit, lender instruction, debt assignment, pledge or settlement.
- No double counting loan proceeds as operating revenue. Issuing a note creates liability, acquiring a note creates an asset with credit risk, acquiring collateral does not erase obligations. Debt proceeds must have a lawful financial source and be evidenced as cleared funds.

## Finance workpaper and evidence required per instrument
1. Legal instrument ID, city SPV, Ontario/other jurisdiction, issuance/transfer date and governing law.
2. Named real parties: maker/drawer, payee, drawee and any acceptor, lender/holder/borrower; KYC and signatory authority.
3. Underlying economic transaction: deed/APS, real principal actually lent, acquired note assignment, issued invoice, accepted service, buyer payment source.
4. Original/recorded documents, lender/factor written terms, chain of title, registered security, first/second priority, defaults, maturity, enforceability memo and insurance where relevant.
5. Payable amount in CAD, rate, simple/compound, payment schedule, maturity, actual cash-to-close, taxes/fees, registration and counsel costs, reserves and fallback.
6. Payer consent/acceptance status, approved payment rail, bank-held/trust-held reconciliation and evidence of actual collected cleared funds.
7. Dispute/dishonour status, recourse and applicable notice time limits — never credited as cash or deemed acquiescence.

## Accounting / decision states
IDEA → COUNTERPARTY VERIFIED → UNDERLYING VALUE/OBLIGATION VERIFIED → CREDIT/SECURITY & TITLE DILIGENCE → LAWYER/SECURITIES CHECK → WRITTEN TERMS → USER APPROVAL/SIGNATURE → CLOSING/ISSUANCE OR TRANSFER → ACTUAL FUNDS SETTLED → SERVICED/RECONCILED → DISCHARGED/REPAID.
Any no/failed acceptance, default, dishonour, potential claim or litigation is a separate LOSS/RISK/RECOVERY state and cannot pass 'FUNDS SETTLED'.

## Internal ownership
- Capital: financing route, cost of capital, lawful exemptions, lender written terms, issuer/investor truth.
- Property: APS, VTB, land title, mortgage lien priority, collateral valuation, assignment conditions.
- Treasury: existing genuine payables/receivables, actual settled cash, trust records, debt ledger, reconciliations.
- Legal: enforceability, securities/mortgage-broker rules, authority, approval, registrations, collections.
- Vendor/Media/Travel: invoices only after contracted and delivered services; rights clearance and cancellation/refund risks.
- Original Ethos Runtime: source-first, do-not-repeat counterparties, evidence-state transitions, alert only for actual signed term sheet, executable source of repayment, user-only gate, payments settled or default affecting capital. No automatic issuance.

## User-only gates
Signing/issuing or endorsing an instrument, purchase/funding, granting security, acquiring a loan/note or legal claim, assuming debt, accepting binding terms, securities filings, public fundraising, identity/bank/credit data, regulated advice, collections/enforcement or registration — always explicit approval and qualified professional review.

**Revenue-control statement:** A contractual promise or draft is not settled cash. Cash is recorded only when verified in an authorized controlled/escrow/trust account, with a separate debt obligation ledger and reconciled liabilities.
