## Sponsor benefits of this control
- **No indiscriminate advance of the whole partner fee.** The agreed releases correspond to observable live-event accomplishments.
- **Music is a deliverable.** The second release requires actual original Carnival Careers music to be confirmed in the show's lineup, not simply an artist's appearance.
- **Completion matters.** The third release does not occur until Hopeton finishes his last song.
- **An evidence record.** The sponsor may receive a mutually authorized non-confidential proof sheet with dates, evidence of all earned releases, and status of any unearned balance.
- **A contingent balance.** If the agreed milestone does not occur, the associated unreleased funds remain unreleased and are handled under signed cancellation/refund instructions. This is a contractual safeguard, not a guarantee that the event, music, or performance will occur.
