- **Payment 1:** The event partner is paid once Hopeton gains authorized entry **at the venue door**.
- **Payment 2:** Once **original CC music** is confirmed in the actual show **audio/set lineup**. Artist-listing alone is insufficient.
- **Payment 3:** Once **Hopeton finishes singing his last song onstage**.
- Actual partner fee and amount of each increment are **TBD by new signed partner agreement**. Sponsor billing may be on a separate schedule. No current booked stage, accepted promoter, secured sponsor money, escrow or future artist/venue rights is assumed.
