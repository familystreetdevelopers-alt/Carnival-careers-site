01 / THE PROPOSITION

A sponsor funds an artist campaign - and
receives a defined marketing package.
The commercial purchase is a documented basket of promotional assets, content production, placements and
measured reporting. A sponsor should know what will be published, where, when, for how long and who must
approve it before transferring money.

The important distinction
CC can propose and contract promotion that CC owns or has cleared. An artist's image, personal endorsement, backstage
access, arena screens, stage announcements, VIP tickets, broadcast placement, merchandise marks and venue product rights
remain conditional until written approvals are obtained.

and itemized.

Not included by default Ownership of artist masters, music rights, guaranteed arena slots, venue sponsorship status, ticket
revenue, unapproved category exclusivity, promoter obligations, sponsorship of other artists or
automatically secured tour dates.

What makes the offer compelling
• An artist-forward cultural story with measurable brand creative, rather than an unpriced donation.

• A clearly bounded campaign whose CC-owned marketing can begin without falsely claiming an arena date.

• A named point of delivery, documented proof, and negotiated make-good or refund remedies if a specific deliverable cannot run.

• A pathway to expand into live events, city episodes, food and music, subject to each actual rights holder.

but records zero confirmed stage bookings in the controlling sponsor model. The past 5 Nov 2026 concert is historical and

11 / CONTRACTING RULES

NO SURPRISES. NO IMPLIED
ENDORSEMENTS.
Scope + identity: Specify actual sponsoring legal entity, billing and public brand; identify CC contracting entity and authorized
signatories. Any specific sponsored artist needs full legal performance name and authorized representative.

Exclusivity: No exclusivity unless specified by product category, conflicts list, platforms, geographic market, term, prior
commitments and license fee; no venue-wide scope unless venue signs.

Artist name/likeness: Use only within exact art, platforms, geography, ad format and term licensed by artist/manager. Paid usage,
whitelisting, artist-channel posts and sponsored endorsements require separate consent.

Music + footage rights: A master recording license does not necessarily include composition sync, publisher, songwriter, performer
or event filming rights. Chain-of-title checks precede publication.

Privacy and endorsements: Obtain consent for filmed participants and children; comply with applicable Canadian marketing,
privacy, competition, platform and disclosure requirements. Sponsored restaurant opinions cannot be scripted as independent
reviews.

Content approval: Sponsor approves its trademarks and factual claims within a finite review window; CC retains editorial/legal
compliance; artist/promoter review only where their rights are used.

Change of artist/date: If an artist, venue or slot changes, no new face or arena is automatically substituted. Use written change
order, specified comparable inventory or refund rights.

Cancellation and force majeure: Separate cancellation triggers for campaign content and any live show; define notice, deposit
treatment, insurance, reasonable mitigation, rescheduling and refund allocation.

Taxes, invoices, escrow: Fees stated in CAD excluding applicable taxes unless expressly included. Payment destination,
trust/escrow if any, disbursement instructions and fees require written verification.

Intellectual property + archival: Sponsor keeps its marks; CC keeps its existing IP; artist keeps its rights. Sponsor has only approved,
time/territory-limited use of delivered media; old social posts/archives may remain under agreed archival terms.

Insurance / brand safety: Appropriate insurance, indemnity allocation, claims review, event security and category restrictions
depend on the deliverable and location.

Evidence and remedies: Require proof per line item, reporting cadence, cure periods, defined replacement/refund rules, dispute
process and termination for material breach.

Sponsor-safe designation
Until any specific talent endorsement is signed, the brand may be called a partner of Carnival Careers' artist-support campaign.
Do not say the artist personally recommends the product, that the arena named the brand its official partner or that a future
performance is confirmed.

RELEASE 2  /  OUR ORIGINAL MUSIC

The second release becomes earned when Carnival Careers' ORIGINAL MUSIC - named CC track(s) - is in the confirmed
event AUDIO / SET LINEUP. Being invited or listed as a performer alone does not satisfy this trigger.

PROOF: Signed/dated stage-manager or show-caller confirmation listing the CC song titles.

RELEASE 3  /  LAST SONG COMPLETED

The event partner earns the final release only once Hopeton sings and completes his last song onstage at the agreed show.
