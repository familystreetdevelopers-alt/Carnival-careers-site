# CARNIVAL CAREERS — SHOWRUNNER / WHOLE UNIVERSE PRODUCTION CONTRACT
**Controlling creative specification · October 14, 2026 · replaces the mistaken “44-day” interpretation**

## ONE UNIVERSE: PRODUCED ENTERTAINMENT + REAL BUSINESS
The “44 minutes of basketball / WWE” image means **meticulous production, beat by beat, minute by minute**. The script controls camera placements, rehearsals, transitions, reveals, performers, releases, stage cues, meals, cruise activities, construction filming and sponsor deliverables. It does **not** impose a 44-day execution deadline or a 44-minute actual show duration. Every project has its own dynamic cue clock and real-world dependency schedule.

The season creates a repeatable city-to-city entertainment franchise where one household's journey can connect: restaurant/family dinner → property acquisition → design + engineer-of-record → OBC/permitting → construction and reveal → neighborhood commerce and food → Caribbean music/festival or guest appearance → cruise/travel experience → edited TV/short-form/behind-the-scenes media → real revenue, expenses and settlement. Different episodes may use a different order or omit unavailable elements.

### THREE PARALLEL CLOCKS — NEVER MERGE THEIR TRUTH
1. **SHOW CLOCK**: editorial runtime and cue-by-cue schedule in minutes/seconds, stage management and contingency cutaways. This is intentionally scripted/choreographed.
2. **DEVELOPMENT CLOCK**: genuine sellers, APS/executable paper, title/survey, TRCA/flood and zoning, architecture, OBC professional engineers, permitted construction, occupancy and household rights. Independent outcomes are not scripted.
3. **CASH CLOCK**: invoices, contractual consideration, signed vendor/sponsor agreements, debt obligations, actual third-party cleared cash, financing draws, escrow and payoffs. A filmed handshake does not move money.

### FARHEATER / ONTARIO ENGINEERING RELEASE
- Architect/project manager supplies measured architectural floor plans, dimensions, elevations/sections, room usages/occupancies, code basis and site survey.
- Structural and geotech/hydro determine actual support, access, elevations, service-entry and risk constraints.
- Engineer of record quotes and develops detailed HVAC, ventilation, electrical load/riser/panels, domestic plumbing/sanitary/storm, sprinkler/hydraulic and fire alarm as required by the Ontario Building Code, current adopted edition, and local authority.
- FarHeater—or the Ontario-qualified professional responsible for each discipline—reviews, coordinates, signs/seals only their own complete permit drawings; conceptual images and DXF cannot be represented as stamped engineering.
- At **98 Euclid** no buildability is confirmed; TRCA floodplain/regulated-area and City review are hard dependencies before deep excavation commitment.

### SCRIPTING / TRUST SAFEGUARDS
- Script **the presentation**, not independently determined financial outcomes or opinions. Re-enactments are labelled and need consent. No staged false endorsements.
- Never represent an investor, lender, seller, engineer, building department, cruise provider, venue, artist or customer as having approved, paid, contracted or delivered without source evidence.
- Do not disguise sponsor influence on a food review or imply a customer booking where there is none.
- Separate modeled finance from quoted, signed, invoiced, received and settled. Independent value appraisal and exit are required; no unverified “contract arbitrage” returns.
- Keep private development deal documents, family files, credit, bank and investor diligence **out of the public repo and site**.
- All signatures, money, bank, personal finance, credit, identity, binding contracts, destructive site access, professional retainer and irreversible actions remain human approved.

### PROGRAMMATIC CUE VALIDATION
A beat can be `SCRIPTED` before anyone is hired. A filmed deliverable becomes `CLEARED` only after proof of booked venue/location rights, participant releases, safety/code constraints, insurance and relevant rights. Acquisition becomes `CLOSED` only after actual conveyance. Debt becomes `REPAID` only when verified actual payment clears. Producer-facing UI is a coordination aid, not a substitute for evidence ledgers.
