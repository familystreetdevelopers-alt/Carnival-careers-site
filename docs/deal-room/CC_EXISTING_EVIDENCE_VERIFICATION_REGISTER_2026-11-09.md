## Signature / acceptance distinctions
A source can have **actual signatures** but still lack authority for the next event, subsequent revised pages, brand inventory or a new artist slot. A signature image copied from a prior agreement does not prove acceptance of a later revised version. Validate signing authority and effective dates before offering a contract as binding.

## Current artist music rights warning
Original recordings/music may be CC-created but recording ownership, publishing, distribution and synchronization/public performance can be licensed or exclusive. The WIFYX/Brand New agreement has relevant language. Before **release 2** is promised as deliverable, identify both CC tracks, publishing and master-control rights, and obtain proper confirmation from an authorized show caller or music director **plus any required music licenses**.

## Defensible document labels
`HISTORICAL_SIGNED`, `ARTIST_ONLY_SIGNED`, `REVISED_ACCEPTANCE_UNVERIFIED`, `WRITTEN_CONDITIONAL_OFFER`, `UNVERIFIED_INVOICE`, `PROPOSED_NEW_CONTRACT`, `CURRENT_EXECUTED`, `CASH_CLEARED`. **Only use the last two after primary-source verification.**

## Before any release
For each attachment: exact source, current/historical, all signatories and revisions, onward-disclosure permission, redacted fields, correct project, rights it actually grants, original checksum and who approved sharing. No general raw-folder access for unrelated recipients.
