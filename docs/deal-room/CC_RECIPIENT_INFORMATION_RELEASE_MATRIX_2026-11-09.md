## Three statuses before documents leave CC control
- **SOURCE VERIFIED:** original file bytes, dates and signatories confirmed; still check onward-disclosure rights.
- **CURRENT & EXECUTED:** both applicable parties accepted current effective version; rights, delivery terms and dates are still valid; no contrary termination.
- **RELEASABLE TO RECIPIENT:** above, plus named purpose, legitimate need, authorization, redactions and any required NDA / data-room access.

An attachment, a preliminary unsigned memo, a signature on a superseded version, a bill not accepted, a quote or a draft invoice **cannot** be upgraded to a confirmed commercial commitment. A source emailed to a party is not proof of acceptance.

## The event-partner's current proposed commercial structure — never misdescribe it
1. **AT THE DOOR:** first payment when Hopeton actually obtains authorized arena entry and necessary credentials.
2. **CC ORIGINAL MUSIC IN EVENT AUDIO LINEUP:** second payment only when named original Carnival Careers tracks are confirmed in the show's actual audio/set list.
3. **HOPETON'S LAST SONG:** third payment only after he sings and finishes his last song onstage.

The promoter/partner fee is a negotiated amount split into **three individually stated releases** in the future signed agreement. Do NOT invent fee sizes or claim the parties have accepted. Brand sponsorship fee and its payment terms are a **different agreement**.

## Approvals and logging
Owner approves: recipient, named entity, exact documents and versions, fields redacted, disclosure purpose, date, sender and expiration/revocation. Authorised counsel verifies third-party confidentiality; private contracts are not copied or publicized indiscriminately. Maintain recipient-level logs with document IDs and status changes; **no automatic contact**.
