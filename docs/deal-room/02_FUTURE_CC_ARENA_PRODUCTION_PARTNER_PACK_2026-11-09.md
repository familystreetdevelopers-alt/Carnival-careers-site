# Carnival Careers — Future CC-Organized Arena Concert: Production Partner Brief
**Audience:** experienced arena producer, co-promoter, tour operator or event-management company able to contract, insure, budget and deliver a separate Carnival Careers show. **Status:** exploratory proposal; not a confirmed venue booking or production partnership.

## This is different from buying a short opening slot
For CC's own show, **CC as proposed organizer** would assume the organizer-defined ticket, sponsorship, vendor, music, venue and operating rights **only under completed agreements and financial controls**. In contrast, for an opening act on an existing promoter's show CC buys a negotiated slot and does not obtain ticket-gross rights from that event by default.

## Partner assignment — allocate named owners before marketing
- **Producer/co-promoter:** event feasibility, venue negotiations, routing, show calendar, deposits, staffing, risk/insurance, permitting, security, lawful supplier approvals and contractual source documents.
- **CC:** series IP/format, original creative, families/food/production storyline, own cleared music, proposed artist relationships, sponsor narrative, projected vendor/media plan, rights clearances it controls.
- **Legal/finance:** one event SPV or contracting entity, deposit custody, separate sponsor orders/invoices, artist guarantees, vendor advances, insurer and ticket settlement.
- **Rights & sponsor team:** venue/artist inventory verification (no inferred VIP, arena signage, F&B or broadcast rights), sponsor statements and reporting.

## What an experienced producer receives
**L0:** executive show brief, draft arena attendance/price scenarios (ESTIMATES), indicative artist mix (not booked), event program outline and how CC originals/food/family show fit the live production.

**L1:** preliminary production budget and scenario P&L, draft set/rider/venue layout, task/production schedule, sponsor tier matrix, supplier quote register, redacted historical signed-memo evidence, signed/unsigned rights and permits matrix, possible multi-city roll-out *only as planned*.

**L2 under authorized diligence:** genuine current vendor quotes or signed supply agreements; historic contract/agent memo source copies with consent and restrictions; actual issuer/payee invoices and paid/unpaid ledgers; selected music licensing/rights; insurance; venue quote/hold/contract; separate original music chain-of-title; financing sources and uses. **Never treat old historical invoices or memos as current contractual receivables.**

## First joint working session — only six decisions
1. Who is the **actual organizer/contracting counterparty** and who has booking authority?
2. Which named **arena/date hold and all-in quote** is attainable?
3. What is the **credible performer billing and actual artist deposit** route?
4. What are **three cash scenarios** after venue, artist, security, production, fee, insurance, taxes and working capital; who bears a downside?
5. Which **sponsor/VIP/vendor rights** does the organizer actually own and can offer?
6. What are the **signed documents, dates, receipts, invoice IDs and next approvals** required before anyone claims the plan is financed?
