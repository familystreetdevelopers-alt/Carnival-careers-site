**Effective:** 2026-11-14 · **Record type:** non-confidential public operations index · **Truth state:** audit in progress

## One composition / multiple recordings
The composition is registered independently of the mix. Keep drafts/stems securely, verify all composer/lyricist splits, check existing society work numbers and any publishing/administration agreement **before** making claims. Final master files should get the correct ISRC and should not be distributed until approved. Do not create a new work/ISRC simply because a mix has been mastered.

## Rights collection — no overlaps
| Income / right | Preferred eligibility route | Register/claim only after |
|---|---|---|
| Songwriter public performance | Existing Canadian SOCAN writer affiliation and reciprocal societies abroad | Compare live SOCAN repertoire, titles, credits and IPI |
| Canadian reproduction / mechanical | SOCAN Reproduction Rights **or** CMRRA, depending on contracts | Check scope by share/territory; avoid double appointments |
| U.S. digital-audio mechanicals | The MLC or **existing authorized** administrator | Confirm independent shares and publisher/TuneCore administration |
| Master sales / interactive streaming | Existing distributor (TuneCore where applicable) | Confirm existing release, ISRC/UPC, owner and audio |
| Canadian performer/master neighbouring rights | Re:Sound directly or an approved member collective | Check eligible repertoire, competing representation and master rights |
| U.S. digital noninteractive sound recording rights | SoundExchange (performer/maker where eligible) | Confirm claimant, ISRC, master owner |
| International performance rights | SOCAN reciprocal-network claims | Do NOT create overlapping ASCAP/BMI/PRS/SACEM/etc writer memberships |
| International neighbouring rights | Verify SoundExchange and Re:Sound reciprocity | Coverage and territorial double-claim review |
| Film/TV/ads/games synchronization | Individually negotiated written music-and-master permissions | All owners cleared; any surviving publishing/exclusive permissions resolved |
| Formal copyright certificates | CIPO / U.S. Copyright Office, optional | Accurate author, claimant, publication status, appropriate fee authority |
| Content ID / UGC | Authorized exclusive eligible master only | No shared nonexclusive beats, samples or competing claims |

## Mandatory status labels
- **SOURCE FOUND:** title/file identified, not necessarily copyright ownership.
- **RIGHTS UNVERIFIED:** author, split or contracts incomplete.
- **READY TO FILE:** verified credits, ownership, no existing duplicate registration.
- **DRAFT SAVED:** authenticated service reports an actual saved draft.
- **SUBMITTED:** official confirmation from the receiving rights organization.
- **REGISTERED:** society-issued work number/confirmation or official copyright certificate.
- **ROYALTIES COLLECTED:** actual deposited payment verified.

## Risks and safeguards
Do not place confidential contracts, old drafts, songwriter percentages, account IDs, finance terms, artist documents or unpublished recordings into this public repository. No registration should assert 100% ownership when the share is unknown. Preserve old nonexclusive beat licences. A publisher/administrator with contractual authority may already collect some mechanical income; audit first.
