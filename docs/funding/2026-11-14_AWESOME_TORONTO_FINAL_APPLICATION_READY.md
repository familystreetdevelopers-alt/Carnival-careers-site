## Tell us about your awesome project! (under 2,000 characters)

Toronto has incredible Caribbean musical and creative talent, but many local residents do not know how everyday jobs in audio production, live-event operations and cultural small businesses work. I want to organize one small, completely free and accessible neighbourhood Creative Careers Discovery Day for local people who are curious about these careers. A working local music/audio professional would demonstrate a safe basic sound check, explain behind-the-scenes roles and answer practical career questions. A short, informal session would give attendees a chance to ask about creative entrepreneurship and community cultural events.

The project would be a single Toronto neighbourhood workshop, not a concert, ticketed event, commercial product launch or television production. Attendance would be free, with a proposed target of 15–20 residents. A small neighbourhood community room or available accessible library room would be requested; no venue or instructor is yet booked and neither will be presented as confirmed. If selected, I would first secure an accessible location and suitable facilitator, then announce free registration. Any optional documentation would require permission from participants and would not be sold or used in a commercial program without consent.

Carnival Careers is a broader independent cultural media concept, but this proposed event is a distinct, community-benefit activity with its own budget and no requirement to purchase tickets or merchandise. Your $1,000 would enable the complete small event rather than serving as a partial down payment for an arena production.
