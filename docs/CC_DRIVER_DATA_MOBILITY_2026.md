## Viral source claim disposition
The "Unethical ways to make money Part 158" claim that the US federal government pays a driver US$3,500 to plug a government-issued OBD2 logger into their personal car and upload weekly routes is **unsubstantiated**. Official SBIR is R&D financing to eligible US-owned for-profit small businesses through competitive solicitations, not a public baseline-contributor driver stipend. Do not use $3,500 as an expected payment in CC, family pitches, grants or proformas.

## Compliance gates
Ontario general minimum wage C$17.95/hr effective Oct 1 2026: https://www.ontario.ca/document/your-guide-employment-standards-act-0/minimum-wage . Third-party postings saying '$15-$18/hr' without currency, type, and lawful wage explanation are not pay commitments.
Ontario fee-based recruitment and temporary help agency services generally require licensing as defined in ESA; new recruiter licence application C$1,500 generally (2026) and separate agency licensing/security may apply: https://www.ontario.ca/document/your-guide-employment-standards-act-0/licensing-temporary-help-agencies-and-recruiters . CC cannot claim a fee until licence status and exemption verified.
All GPS/dashcam/OBD2 personal information needs appropriate lawful collection, meaningful and revocable consent where required, transparent usage/licensing, security and retention. Protect passenger/child location. No OBD installation based solely on a viral video. Check vehicle owner and insurer permission. Confirm safe driving licence status; no participation under a suspension or unsafe condition.
