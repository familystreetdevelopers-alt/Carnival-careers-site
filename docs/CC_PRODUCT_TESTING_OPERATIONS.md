# CC Product Testing & Brand Research

Toronto-first Commerce / Family Wealth / Brand Services lane.

## Services
- Paid private product usability testing and structured feedback supplied to contracting manufacturers and merchants.
- Original UGC demonstrations, unboxings, product photography and licensed advertising assets, with clear paid/free-product disclosures where published.
- Optional research participant recruitment through authorized programs and consented processes.
- Inventory resale only when CC owns the goods and the supply contract permits resale.

## Hard rules
- NO purchased, reimbursed, discounted or incentivized Amazon customer reviews. No review swaps, AMZ review groups, review manipulation, or indirect compensation. Amazon Vine is invitation-only and separate.
- No automatic signup, fake participant profiles, scraping private communities, automation contrary to program terms, undisclosed endorsements, medical or safety performance claims, or posting testimonials without genuine experience.
- Never resell products from BzzAgent, Influenster, or Rebaid; each restricts resale. Rebaid is US-shopper-only. Do not assume other sampled items are transferable; verify each campaign.
- Do not accept paid briefs, contracts, account terms, ID/tax/bank requests, buy items, or grant content rights without user approval when binding.
- Do not automatically represent tenant-owners as qualified/consenting testers. Adult opt-in and work contracts required.

Cash = contract fee + separately authorized lawful resale proceeds - landed testing/production/shipping/fees/tax/returns; free-product retail value is NOT earned cash.
