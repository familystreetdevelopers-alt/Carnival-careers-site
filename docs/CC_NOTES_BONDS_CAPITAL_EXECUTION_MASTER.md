## EXECUTIVE OPERATING RULE
The quickest credible capital is underwritten against **owned/controlled collateral and signed third-party cash receivables**. A promissory note is a borrower's enforceable promise, not money CC can "cash" from thin air. An unaccepted draft/dishonour never creates collateral or cash. An investor bond and a project completion/surety bond are different instruments: first may be a regulated security; second is a risk-transfer guarantee and not itself a cash loan.

**Do not issue, distribute, market returns, sell notes/bonds, solicit retail investors, pledge IP/property, sign, remit personal finance docs or take collateral without actual owner approval and Canadian securities/mortgage legal review.** FSRA: https://www.fsrao.ca/industry/mortgage-brokering/compliance-and-other-resources/promissory-notes-may-be-securities-regulated-ontario-securities-commission-what-you-need-know ; enforcement reminder: https://www.fsrao.ca/industry/mortgage-brokering/regulatory-framework/supervision/dealing-promissory-notes-could-land-you-hot-water-osc-and-fsra .

### 2. TV, documentary, music videos, IP and episodes
