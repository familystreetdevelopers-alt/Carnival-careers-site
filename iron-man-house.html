The Houses · Toronto prototype · 35 Tournament Drive

Iron Man
House.

A bold, technology-forward vision for turning an existing Toronto duplex into four homes plus a proposed backyard garden suite — five residences if all required approvals are obtained.

A Carnival Careers tenant-owner property concept. This is an illustrative design story, not approved construction drawings.

CONCEPT STUDY · NOT TO SCALE
The transformation

One site. Two buildings. A new ownership pathway.

The working plan retains and improves the principal building, proposes two additional homes within its fourplex conversion, and adds a distinct garden suite where municipal requirements permit. Tenant-owner occupancy, financing, and the build sequence are separate approvals — none are represented as complete.

Inside the plan.

Select a view to see the proposed functions. This is an interactive program overview, not a measured floor plan or a construction-ready 3D model.

The street-facing house.

The architecture aims for a powerful dark/red exterior language, four independently functioning homes, sensible entries and services, and a building envelope that meets the final engineer and municipality requirements. Interior room assignments and floor layouts remain to be verified against survey and code.

The residence behind it.

A detached garden suite creates a possible fifth home without relying on an unverified unit-count assumption. It requires independent analysis of lot geometry, setbacks, utility capacity, drainage, emergency access and the City's current garden-suite rules.

Draw. Approve. Finance. Build.

Start with seller control, current survey and measured as-builts. Coordinate architectural, structural, mechanical, electrical, plumbing and fire-safety design; verify permits and lender terms; then undertake construction and tenant-owner occupancy only on an approved schedule.
