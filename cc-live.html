How a paid partnership begins

  1. Request the package and agree on audience, schedule, platform, content approvals and rights.
  2. Sign an insertion order stating exactly what CC can deliver, with cancellation and make-good rules.
  3. Receive a verified invoice or protected payment link directly from Carnival Careers.
  4. Run the disclosed sponsored session, document deliverables and reconcile actual payment.
No guarantee of a specific view count or financial return. Sponsor logos, artist footage, game footage and licensed music are used only with permission. No arena access, tournament prizes or guest appearances are included unless separately signed.
