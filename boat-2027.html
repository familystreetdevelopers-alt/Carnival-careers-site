Real route procurement

Three possible ways to fulfill the journey.

A / PROPOSED EXACT ROUTE

Same-vessel corridor

One ship sailing the complete Toronto–Montréal–Miami–Caribbean–Toronto itinerary, with every port, capacity, duration, return and Canadian/U.S. rule confirmed by the operator in writing. No quote yet.

B / REPOSITIONING

Move the timing

Use the closest commercially viable 2027 southbound itinerary if May cannot support the full route. Dates and ports would require a revised signed offer.

C / MAY FALLBACK

Toronto launch + Caribbean sailing

Toronto/Montréal cultural experience, licensed travel to a Florida/Caribbean departure, operator-approved Caribbean cruise, and separately booked return to Toronto. Not the same as a continuous ship voyage.

Fulfillment / one responsibility per lane

Culture, travel sales and marine operations stay separate.

RASTA FEST / CRMA — CULTURAL LEAD

Proposed creative direction, artist/community relationships and cultural programming; authority and rights to be governed by its signed agreement with Carnival Careers.

CC UNIVERSE — COMMERCIAL & MEDIA

Operator procurement support, sponsor packages, production coordination, communications and media development, subject to signed contributions and rights.

AMSHKA — LIFESTYLE / MARKETPLACE

Proposed vendor, lounge and island-linked activation coordinator. No operator appointment, travel-selling agency or promised onboard space.

VESSEL OPERATOR — ACTUAL SAILING

Ship, crew, safety, port operations, cabins, catering, onboard venue permissions, itinerary and marine-law obligations under the charter/group contract.

TICO-REGISTERED TRAVEL SELLER — GUESTS & MONEY

Guest quote, compliant sale, contract, consumer disclosures, permitted payment method, trust accounting, supplier remittance, bookings, documents, cancellations and refunds.

PORT & LOCAL VENDORS — AUTHORIZED DELIVERY

Approved shore vendors, transfers, excursion suppliers, venue permits, insurance, production load-in and agreed arrival/return windows.

Vendor / marketplace structure

Three participation lanes, each with separate permissions.

ONBOARD / CONDITIONAL

Selective experiences

Curated brand moments, lounges, licensed merchandise or filming only if the operator approves the actual footprint and category. This is not an arena trade-show floor.

SHORE / PERMIT-BASED

Island-linked marketplaces

Local Caribbean vendors, shore hospitality, food, arts and partner activations where venues, customs, business licences and insurance allow.

DIGITAL / APPROVED

Commerce after the voyage

Approved merchandise, QR catalogs, pre-order fulfillment, branded media or sponsor placements without assuming a permanent onboard booth.

No automatic transfer of Taboo booth pricing or 75 Scotiabank Arena vendor spots onto a 190–210-guest cruise ship. Every vendor fee, commission, footprint and cancellation term belongs in a signed schedule.

Decision gates / no premature financial exposure

Confirm delivery before collecting travel money.

1 — PROJECT RIGHTS

Rasta Fest / CC operating agreement; Amshka scope and approvals.

2 — ROUTE / SHIP

Operator’s route A/B/C quote, dates, flag, inventory, attrition, cancellation and ports.

3 — REGULATORY & SELLER

Canadian coasting-trade assessment, licensed travel retailer/wholesaler, trust-account and merchant structure.

4 — SPONSORS & CAPITAL

Actual signed/collectible sponsorship; budget reserve and authority over non-refundable releases.

5 — VENDORS & TALENT

Permits, insurance, ship/local rights, contracts and appropriate artist/media releases.

6 — PUBLIC BOOKINGS

Seller issues travel offers only after G1–G5, including full guest cancellation and itinerary disclosures.
