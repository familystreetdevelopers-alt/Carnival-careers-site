What changes—and what stays

Keep the cultural reach

London and Tokyo still appear in the show. Their pilot acquisitions are deferred; local production/property partners would provide access, subject to priced contracts, insurance and filming rights.

Ghana stays in the pilot. The two proposed extra apartments are paused pending legal construction approval and bids; nine existing modeled units remain.

Keep the six-family commitment

Six Toronto tenant-owner households remain. Each has a proposed C$800 monthly ownership-affordability goal, subject to mortgage and subsidy underwriting. A lawful 4-bedroom-plus family home is essential.

Owner-occupied homes are excluded from market-rent NOI. PATH and East Bayfront condos are already tenanted.
