How funded assignments work

There is no single universal “Legal Aid for nannies” that independent caregivers can join to receive government pay on demand. Which payment route applies depends on the family, the service arrangement and the approved provider.

Licensed-agency / subsidy route

Ontario Child Care Fee Subsidy, and CWELCC where applicable, can support eligible family care in an approved licensed program. In-home providers must be supervised by a licensed home child care agency where the licensed framework applies.

Find a licensed Toronto agency ↗

Ontario Works care support

Some Ontario Works families can get help with childcare needed for employment or approved participation. Certain informal care costs have limits and require authorization or documentation. The family/caseworker determines eligibility, not Sideline Sitters.

Read Ontario Works policy ↗

Private / employer-funded work

Independent family or employer-paid arrangements are separate from public subsidy. Terms, safety checks, employment classification, rates and insurance must be confirmed before a booking.
