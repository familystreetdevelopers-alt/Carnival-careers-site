The CC operating system

From verified opportunity to cleared payment

01 · VERIFY

Match an official employer or university page to a live, compensated project.

02 · QUALIFY

Driver applies directly; company verifies licence, age, right to work and insurance.

03 · CONTRACT

Review exact payout, scheduled hours, equipment, privacy and opt-out terms.

04 · DELIVER

Perform safe, consented driving; send data only through approved channels.

05 · RECONCILE

Verify pay received; CC books only funds it is legally entitled to earn.

CC Families: opportunity referral and household employment tracking. CC Mobility: testing, field-data jobs and contracts. CC Operations: employer due diligence and insurance/privacy approvals. CC Capital: contract-backed revenue, payroll, taxes and confirmed receipts — no fictional government grants.

Required safety and business controls

Never mount a logger, camera, or tracking app in a vehicle without an approved study/contract, clearly documented informed consent, appropriate owner/lessee and insurer permission, and a credible privacy-security policy. Trip logs can expose homes, workplaces, passengers and children. Do not film, record or sell personal route data of other people without lawful authority. Check licence status and insurance before any driving. In Ontario, charging an employer for recruitment or staffing ordinarily requires the relevant licence; community members must not be charged placement fees. CC does not claim that licence has been obtained.
