Paper is not payment.

Legitimate loans, performing mortgage notes, seller take-back debt and properly accepted commercial payment instruments can finance real work. A signature, unaccepted draft or dishonour cannot create cash without an underlying legal obligation and an actual source of funds.

What the instruments actually mean

Promise. Order. Security. Settlement.

Promissory note

A maker promises to pay.

Evidence of an agreed monetary obligation to an actual lender or other payee. Payment terms must be ascertainable; creditworthiness and collateral, if any, need proof.

Bill of exchange / draft

A drawer orders payment.

A real drawee is addressed to pay the payee. The drawee does not become liable on an unaccepted bill just because the drawer wrote it. Approval, acceptance and actual settlement are distinct.

Bond / investment note

A regulated financing obligation.

Corporate debt securities require careful legal classification, offering and registration/exemption analysis. Neither a bond nor an investment note is inherently safe.

Secured mortgage obligation

A real debt with genuine collateral.

A mortgage or other registered security may support repayment, but title, lien priority, valuation, assignments, enforcement costs and borrower protections all matter.

Canada's rule is not “dishonour funds the draft.”

Dishonour can trigger recourse against parties already liable on a genuine bill, with statutory notice rules where applicable. It does not force an unrelated bank, government, court or company to honour a unilateral demand or create money in an account.

Integrated into the existing CC capital stack

Four lawful deal lanes.

01 · Property / VTB

Vendor take-back notes

Negotiate an actual deferred purchase price with the seller. Counsel documents amount, interest, dates, down payment, senior financing, mortgage/charge registration, lien rank and buyer obligations. A contemplated VTB is not a funded acquisition.

02 · Notes as assets

Purchase performing mortgage debt

Only after a complete chain of ownership, note/charge documents, payment history, enforceable assignment, verified borrower obligations, servicing plan, true outstanding balance and downside model.

03 · Commerce / receivables

Get paid for delivered work

Vendor, sponsor, media and product services become eligible receivables only when an actual customer owes an enforceable payment. Authorized factoring/assignment can be reviewed separately after invoice verification.

04 · Treasury

Use real settlement rails

Accept genuine lender-funded proceeds, permitted bank transfers, trust/escrow disbursements or bank-approved drafts. Book cash only after verified clearing and reconciliation; debt proceeds remain liabilities, not earned revenue.

One universal control path

No fictional money.

Verify

Real counterparty, genuine debt or earned receivable, title, signatures and authority.

Price

Underlying amount, collateral, rates, repayment capacity, taxes, legal/security and closing costs.

Approve

Counsel/regulatory review, written terms and explicit user approval before binding acts.

Reconcile

Closing/assignment, settled cash, a separate debt ledger, servicing and discharge.
