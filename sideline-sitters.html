How it will work

One clear path from
caregiver to paid care.

Not a directory of names. The operating model is a managed service: verified caregivers, approved care arrangements, real shifts, documented attendance and a responsible payment source.

01 / INTRODUCE YOURSELF

Join the portal

Start with basic role, coverage-area and experience information. No sensitive identity documents are collected on this public website.

02 / HUMAN REVIEW

Get qualified

Credential checks, references, CPR / first aid and the appropriate agency or independent-care approvals are reviewed by authorized people.

03 / LIVE SERVICE

Accept real work

Once operating agreements and booking systems are activated, approved caregivers can be offered shifts suited to their availability and credentials.

Funding routes — not funding promises

Private bookings and approved subsidies. Two distinct tracks.

Our aim is to make childcare easier to access and easier to provide. A government-supported booking can be offered only through the actual eligible family, program and licensed provider arrangement.

Check caregiver pathways →
Licensed-agency pathwayOntario childcare fee subsidies and CWELCC can apply through approved licensed childcare programs and supervised agency arrangements — not automatically to an independent nanny.
Ontario Works pathwayCertain approved childcare costs, including informal care in qualifying employment-related situations, may be reimbursed or treated as an earnings deduction. Family/caseworker eligibility rules apply.
Private, employer and sponsor fundedFamilies or approved purchasers contract and pay for properly arranged care. A separate route from government-program participation.
