Carnival Careers is acquiring income-producing real estate, developing affordable family ownership opportunities, and buying selected properties for profitable resale.

Our financing strategy is simple: investors provide the minimum money needed for acquisitions and closings. Banks finance the properties and construction. Rental income services the long-term mortgages. Property resales and refinancings provide additional opportunities to repay investor capital.

We are not asking one investor to pay for the entire development upfront.

04The six-family housing commitment

Six families must receive suitable housing, with a real path to ownership.

Two families will purchase VicTowns homes. Two will become owners at Iron Man House, and two at CaveMansion.

One family requires at least four bedrooms. Tournament provides the proposed initial accommodation, and CaveMansion will provide its permanent four-bedroom home.

The remaining VicTowns properties provide transitional housing and resale opportunities. No family is displaced before suitable replacement accommodation is secured.

Our family homeownership program targets $800 monthly mortgage payments, supported by qualifying mortgages and properly funded shared-equity arrangements.

The PATH and East Bayfront condominiums remain occupied by their existing market-rent tenants.

10The investment opportunity

Carnival Careers is seeking C$2.35 million to execute its first income-producing acquisition at 109 Pembroke Street, subject to mortgage qualification.

The investor return objective is C$3.55 million, comprising C$2.35 million in returned capital and C$1.2 million in profit.

The strategy is to use the smallest practical investor contribution, finance property development through approved institutional and asset-based lending, allow rental income to service long-term mortgages, and supplement investor repayment through six VicTowns property resales and property equity realization.

The result we are building toward is a portfolio of cash-flowing properties, six affordable family homeownership opportunities, two downtown rental condominiums, and recurring media and commercial opportunities connected to the Carnival Careers universe.

The opportunity is asset-backed, staged and designed for long-term profitability—not dependent on an investor funding the entire development at once.

All figures are Canadian dollars. Property purchase prices, financing, construction, rental income and resale profits are modeled or proposed until independently verified.
