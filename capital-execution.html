Finance by the real revenue/asset

Seven executable credit structures.

01 · Live event

Stage access + sponsor bridge

A short-term facility needs documented event-stage permissions, verifiable delivery cost, an accepted sponsor obligation and credible fallback repayment.

Terms only · promoter and sponsor paper not signed
02 · Vendor marketplace

Trade and invoice funding

Creditworthy signed customer contracts and earned invoices may support trade credit, factoring or working capital after lender verification. Targets and unenforceable proposals are not accounts receivable.

Operating target · no lender borrowing base confirmed
03 · Property

Senior mortgage + VTB note

Seller-deferral papers supplement real title, appraised value, buyer equity, senior lender permission and documented mortgage priority. The promissory note records a debt; it is not substitute cash.

Seller and lender terms required
04 · CaveMansion

Acquisition + construction draws

Collateral and lender-controlled construction draws require site acquisition/control, survey, engineering, pricing, title, geotech, permitting and contractor execution documents.

98 Euclid · candidate only, unacquired
05 · Media

Presales, credits + completion bond

Executed minimum guarantees, eligible certified tax credits or assignable distribution receivables may be underwritten. A completion guarantee manages completion risk, not cash proceeds.

No signed collateral represented
06 · Debt assets

Performing mortgage notes

True note purchases rely on legal holder assignments, registered mortgage security, borrower payment histories and independent risk pricing. The purchaser owns a debt asset, not a banking licence.

Legal transfer and servicing proof required
07 · Commerce + travel

Earned receivables / equipment

Actual invoiced business services, authentic owned goods, and secured equipment can support narrower finance products; speculative bookings, passenger deposits and airline credits do not.

Underlying paid work and contracts required

Money does not land because a note exists. Funding occurs only when a real lender accepts the credit, signs an agreement, meets conditions precedent and disburses cleared proceeds to an authorized account. Even a signed sponsor agreement has collection and performance risk.
Interest-rate compliance comes before any borrowing note. A prior example of C$30,000 principal and C$6,000 financing premium due within roughly 24 days has been withdrawn: the short-term annualized cost raises significant Criminal Code section 347 concerns. A qualifying corporate commercial loan of more than C$10,000 to C$500,000 receives the federal commercial-loan exception only when the actuarial APR is at most 48%, including legally relevant fees. From paper to keys

One evidence ladder for every closing.

01 · SOURCEVerified asset or buyer

Title, legal authorization, actual contract and borrower entity.

02 · UNDERWRITELender credit decision

Cash flows, equity, debt covenants, fees, priority and recourse.

03 · EXECUTESignature + funds

Professional review, explicit authority, signed terms and settled money.

04 · DELIVERKeys / stage / invoice

Closing receipts, possession, authorized stage performance or contracted work, then repayment.

Regulatory and transaction safeguards. In Ontario, bonds and many promissory notes are securities; public sale or placement requires counsel and applicable registration/exemption review. A commercial lender's bilateral loan documentation is different from a retail investment promotion. No one can issue a draft to compel an unauthorized third party to pay, or turn dishonour into funding.
